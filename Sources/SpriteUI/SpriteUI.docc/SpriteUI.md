# ``SpriteUI``

Make 2D games with entities and components, render them with SpriteKit, and put
them in your SwiftUI app.

## Overview

A SpriteUI scene is a collection of entities: your player, a wall, a coin, or a
camera. Components describe what each entity has. A sprite component gives it
an appearance; a physics component makes it fall and collide. Systems apply the
rules of your game to the entities that need them.

You do not need to learn every part of the engine before making a game.

### Start learning

Read <doc:getting-started> to display your first sprite. Then follow
<doc:spriteui-tutorials>, a five-lesson course that explains how scenes, components,
systems, input, and levels fit together. You will change working examples and
see what each change does on screen.

Use <doc:api-reference> when you need the details of a particular type. Use the
guides below when your game needs a specific feature.

### Explore a sample game

DemoBots is Apple's sample game rebuilt with SpriteUI. It is a way to explore a
larger app after learning the library, rather than a lesson in the course.
<doc:demobots-cooperative-multiplayer> explains where to find it and how its
parts use SpriteUI. Apple's original assets retain their own license.
